Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Questions

How much does house construction cost in Chennai?
Construction rates depend on specifications, architectural design, soil conditions, and your selected package. Our standard base packages start from ₹2,250/sq.ft for Super, ₹2,450/sq.ft for Deluxe, up to ₹2,650/sq.ft for Premium luxury finishes.
What is included in house construction?
Inclusions depend on the selected package and scope of work agreed upon. Generally, complete house construction covers foundation earthwork, RCC framed structures, brick masonry, plastering, plumbing lines, electrical wiring, tiling, painting, and fitting installation.
Do you provide 2D house plans?
Yes, subject to the service scope agreed for your project. We prepare comprehensive 2D CAD architectural plans optimized for natural lighting, ventilation, and plot dimensions.
Do you provide 3D elevation designs?
Yes, our civil and architectural design team develops photorealistic 3D exterior elevations so you can finalize colours, parapet designs, and facade details before execution begins.
Do you undertake renovation?
Yes. We handle structural retrofitting, additional floor extensions, bathroom/kitchen overhauls, and complete facade renovations for existing residential properties.
Do you construct houses in Tiruvannamalai?
Yes. Tiruvannamalai is one of our primary operating hubs alongside Chennai. We provide complete turnkey construction and site guidance across the region.
Can I build a house if I already have a plan?
Yes. VSHN Builders can review your existing drawing, conduct a site verification, assess practical structural execution feasibility, and provide a clear material & labour estimate.
How do I get a construction estimate?
Simply submit the consultation form below or send us a WhatsApp message with your Location, Plot Size, Number of Floors, and Approximate Built-up Area. We will arrange a consultation and prepare a preliminary estimate.
